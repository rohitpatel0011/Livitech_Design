# LIVITECH DESIGN — DESIGN SYSTEM & COLOR PROTOCOL (DESIGN.md)

## 1. Core Palette Tokens
- **Obsidian Espresso (`#1A120E` / `text-ink` / `bg-ink`):** Primary headings, dark feature containers, image gradient overlays, and footer surface.
- **Deep Espresso (`#3A2418` / `bg-brand-dark`):** Primary button hover states, active pill backgrounds, and high-contrast badges.
- **Architectural Bronze (`#6B4A2E` / `bg-brand` / `text-brand`):** Primary CTA buttons, active tabs, checkmarks, icons, and interactive links.
- **Warm Sandstone (`#A88462` / `text-brand-accent` / `border-brand-accent`):** Secondary highlights, step numbers, active card outlines, and subtle decorative accents.
- **Soft Travertine (`#D5C4AE` / `bg-brand-light` / `border-border`):** 1px card borders, badge backgrounds (`bg-brand-light/40`), section dividers, and inactive pill hover indicators.
- **Warm Slate (`#4A4A46` / `text-ink-muted`):** All body paragraphs, descriptions, metadata, and navigation links.

## 2. Surface & Contrast Rules (60-30-10 Rule)
- **60% Clean Light Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#FAF7F2`):** Keep main backgrounds and card interiors bright so architectural renders and site construction photos remain crisp.
- **30% Structural Anchors (`#1A120E`, `#4A4A46`, `#D5C4AE`):** Typography, borders, and dark contrast blocks (e.g., Callback Form left panel & Footer).
- **10% Action & Brand Accents (`#6B4A2E`, `#3A2418`, `#A88462`):** Strictly reserved for buttons, sliders, badges, and interactive indicators.
